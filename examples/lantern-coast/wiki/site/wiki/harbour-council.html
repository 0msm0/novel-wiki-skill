Harbour Council

The governing council of Port Hallow's harbour.

The Harbour Council governs the harbour of Port Hallow; the Grey Wardens keep the harbour on its behalf.

Overview

The Council governs the harbour of Port Hallow. Years are reckoned "by the Council's count", in which Ilith sank in the Year 412, three hundred years ago.

Background

According to the common account, the Council founded the Grey Wardens about two hundred years ago, after the Drowning, to keep the harbour.

History

Episode 3 What the Water Keeps

Mentioned as the source of the year count and as the founder of the Wardens.
