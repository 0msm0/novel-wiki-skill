Patience

Salt barge that carries Mira and Seren out of Port Hallow.

The Patience is a salt barge mastered by Old Bettany, which carries Mira Osk and Seren of Esk out of Port Hallow.

Description

A flat black hull that sits low in the water, heaped with sacks of salt under tarpaulin, with a rudder chain at the stern. It sails from the Weir landing at the second bell.

History

Episode 2 The Salt Barge

The Patience left the Weir landing at the second bell and escaped the harbour when Seren of Esk drained the water beneath the Gull Gate chain.

Episode 3 What the Water Keeps

The Patience reached Ilith on the third day and drifted, then anchored, over the drowned city. At the end of the episode Mira began taking in the anchor rope.
