Esk

Northern islands said to be home to tidecallers.

Esk is a group of northern islands, the home of Seren of Esk. Its people are said to be able to call the tide.

Culture / Society

According to Tobin Reed's gran, Esk folk can call the tide; Seren confirms that Tidecalling is real. Esk keeps its own account of the fall of Ilith: "the city drowned itself".
