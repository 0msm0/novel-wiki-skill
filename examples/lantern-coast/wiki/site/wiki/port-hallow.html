Port Hallow

Harbour city on the Lantern Coast, Mira Osk's home.

Port Hallow is a harbour city on the Lantern Coast, policed by the Grey Wardens on behalf of the Harbour Council. It is the home of Mira Osk.

Description

Port Hallow stinks of salt and lamp oil. At dusk, lantern-men climb their ladders to light the lamps along the coast, and crowds stop to watch.

History in the story

Episode 1 The Brass Thief

Mira Osk robbed a Warden captain on the Fishmarket Stair, and the harbour was closed hours early that night.

Episode 2 The Salt Barge

Seren of Esk drained the harbour to let the Patience escape; a Warden cutter was smashed against the harbour wall.

Episode 3 What the Water Keeps

Both compasses swung north to point back toward the city.
