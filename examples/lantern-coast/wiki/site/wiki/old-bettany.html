Old Bettany

One-eyed master of the salt barge Patience.

Old Bettany is the one-eyed master of the salt barge Patience, which carries Mira Osk out of Port Hallow.

Appearance

Bettany is a one-eyed woman.

Personality

Discreet and unintimidated: she takes payment without asking questions and spits over the side at a Warden captain. She is practical in a crisis and holds to what "everyone knows".

History

Episode 2 The Salt Barge

Bettany took Mira's last three silver for passage without asking what was in the oilcloth. Confronted by Varr Dane, she insisted she carried only salt.

Episode 3 What the Water Keeps

Bettany dropped the sail to let the Patience drift over Ilith and hauled Mira back aboard after her dive. She countered Mira's claim about the Wardens by saying they were two hundred years old, founded by the Council after the Drowning.
