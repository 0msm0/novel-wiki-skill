Ada Osk

Mira Osk's late mother.

Ada Osk was the mother of Mira Osk. She died when her stall caught fire.

Background

Ada kept a stall that caught fire one night; she did not come out. Her daughter Mira was burned on the right palm that night.

History

Episode 1 The Brass Thief

Mira remembered the night Ada's stall burned and Ada did not come out.

Episode 2 The Salt Barge

Varr Dane claimed to have known Ada, though Mira had never told anyone her mother's name in the city.
