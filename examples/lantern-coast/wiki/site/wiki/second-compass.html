Second brass compass

A twin of the brass compass, found in drowned Ilith.

The second brass compass is an instrument identical to Mira Osk's Brass compass, found on a desk in the drowned bell tower of Ilith.

Description

Identical to the first brass compass, except that its glass is whole.

Powers & properties

  • On the desk in Ilith its needle pointed up at Mira through the water.
  • Placed beside the first compass, the two swung together and pointed north, toward Port Hallow and the Gull Gate.

Background

It lay on a writing desk in the bell tower of Ilith, beside a lantern-man's pole with the Grey Wardens' silver hook worked into its handle.

History

Episode 3 What the Water Keeps

Mira Osk found it in the bell tower and brought it up. Laid beside the first compass on the deck of the Patience, it swung with it to point north toward Port Hallow.
