Pell

Silent deckhand on the salt barge Patience.

Pell is a silent boy who works as deckhand aboard the salt barge Patience under Old Bettany.

Appearance

Pell is a boy who wore a cap with a gull feather in it, until he lost it during the escape from Port Hallow.

Personality

Silent; he works without speaking and watches closely.

History

Episode 2 The Salt Barge

Pell coiled rope and stared at Mira's compass bundle as she came aboard. After the escape he stood at the stern watching the wreck of the Warden cutter. When he turned, his gull-feather cap was gone, and Mira Osk alone saw a silver hook, the emblem of the Grey Wardens, pinned inside his collar.
