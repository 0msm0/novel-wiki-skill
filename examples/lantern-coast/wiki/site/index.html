Episode 3: What the Water Keeps

The Patience finds sunken Ilith, and Seren of Esk tells the Esk account of how its tidecallers drowned the city. Seren cannot remember writing the unsigned letter. Mira Osk dives into the drowned bell tower and recovers a second brass compass lying beside a pole bearing the Grey Wardens' silver hook; both compasses then point back toward Port Hallow, where Tobin Reed is to hang in six days.
