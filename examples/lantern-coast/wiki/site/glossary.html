Glossary

Council's count
The Harbour Council's reckoning of years; Ilith sank in its Year 412, three hundred years ago. See Harbour Council.
Ilithi
The people of Ilith; in the Esk telling, all of them were tidecallers. See Ilith.
Lantern-men
Workers who climb ladders at dusk to light the lamps along the Lantern Coast. See Lantern Coast.
Second bell
A time of night marked by the harbour bells; the salt barge leaves the Weir landing at the second bell. See Port Hallow.
Silver hook
The emblem of the Grey Wardens, stitched on the collars of their grey coats. See Grey Wardens.
The Drowning
A past event after which, by common account, the Harbour Council founded the Grey Wardens about two hundred years ago. See Grey Wardens.
Tidecaller
One who practises tidecalling; associated with the islands of Esk. See Tidecalling.
Warden cutter
A patrol boat of the Grey Wardens. See Grey Wardens.
